## What AGCO actually publishes

One machine-readable contract is publicly reachable: the **AGCO Technical Support (ATS) API**, a
Swagger 2.0 document served at https://secure.agco-ats.com/swagger/docs/v1 with a public Swagger UI
at https://secure.agco-ats.com/swagger. 167 paths, 285 operations, 166 definitions, 55 tags. It is
the platform behind AGCO's Electronic Diagnostic Tool (EDT) and its dealer software distribution
system — not a telematics API. Calling it needs credentials from the AGCO EDT Support Portal
(EDT_Support@agcocorp.com); the Swagger document itself is anonymous.

AGCO also publishes three normative **JSON:API profiles** of its own — Filtering, Search and Change
Events — at https://github.com/agco/agco-json-api-profiles.
